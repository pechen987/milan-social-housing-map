## Review order

1. Resolve street-only positions; withhold new street-only matches.
2. Resolve Paris programme-to-building relationships before counting buildings or summing homes.
3. Match Rome property assets and civic-address datasets to building identities; a property can be one flat or several buildings.
4. Check repeated addresses and coincident coordinates using source IDs and footprints; never jitter coordinates or merge on distance alone.
5. Expand the five cities initially lacking mapped data, starting with Turin’s documented building. Research every district and provider, including negative search results.

The machine-readable `entity-audit.json` lists stable record IDs for collision and street-position review. Legacy records remain visible and explicitly labelled during migration. Only source-verified buildings may be added as new mapped records under v1; unresolved candidates belong in source research logs.
