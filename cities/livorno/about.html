CASALP social-housing addresses in Livorno.

This map transcribes residential entries in CASALP’s December 2025 property inventory ↗. It includes ERP homes, CASALP-owned homes, municipal non-ERP homes and municipal agreed-rent homes whose locality is recorded as Livorno.

How to read the points

Each point is a distinct civic address, geocoded from the published address. Many homes may share one point; the total is therefore an address count, not a dwelling count. The location card retains the property code(s) and the register category, so every point can be audited against the source.

Coverage and limits

The inventory records 6,141 residential units in the included categories at 1,007 distinct Livorno address strings. It excludes garages, commercial premises, land, ancillary units and properties where the inventory records another locality. It is a published property snapshot, not a live tenant or availability list.
