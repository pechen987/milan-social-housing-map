Published register · December 2025

CASALP social-housing addresses in Livorno.

This map transcribes residential entries in CASALP’s December 2025 property inventory ↗. It includes ERP homes, CASALP-owned homes, municipal non-ERP homes and municipal agreed-rent homes whose locality is recorded as Livorno.

How to read the points

Each point is a distinct civic address for which the map geocoder returned a civic-number coordinate. Many homes may share one point; the total is therefore an address count, not a dwelling count. The location card retains the property code(s) and the register category, so every point can be audited against the source.

Coverage and limits

The inventory records 6,141 residential units in the included categories at 1,007 distinct Livorno address strings. This edition maps the 161 addresses that could be placed at a civic-number coordinate. The remaining addresses are retained in the source transcription and will be added only after they can be matched to a building or official civic dataset. It excludes garages, commercial premises, land, ancillary units and properties where the inventory records another locality. It is a published property snapshot, not a live tenant or availability list.
