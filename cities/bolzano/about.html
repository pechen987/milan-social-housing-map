Evidence & method · Compiled October 2026

Bolzano municipal housing.

This release maps 35 documented civic addresses in the Edilizia abitativa section of the Comune di Bolzano’s public property register as at 31 December 2023. They are municipal residential assets, including protected homes for older people where the register identifies them.

How locations were assembled

The public register names 64 civic addresses. 45 were matched to coordinates and are mapped here; the 29 remaining entries are retained in the source folder pending an exact coordinate match. Each map point was transcribed from the register’s residential-housing section and geocoded to a map point. A marker represents a listed civic address, not a count of apartments, vacant homes or individual households.

Coverage limits

  • This is not a complete inventory of social housing in Bolzano.
  • It does not include the full IPES/WOBI portfolio, co-operatives, privately owned subsidised housing, or assets that are not listed under the municipal residential-housing heading.
  • The source is a 2023 asset register; ownership, use and management can have changed since then.
