Evidence & method · October 2026

Documented ERP housing in Genoa.

This release maps 31 address locations from the Comune di Genova's June 2026 list of municipal ERP housing managed by ARTE. Each is joined to the Comune's official civic-address points.

Coverage limits

The published register contains 67 address entries. Thirty-one could be matched unambiguously to a current municipal civic point and appear here; unmatched entries are deliberately withheld. Locations represent recorded addresses, not individual apartments, vacancies or a full citywide housing census.
