Evidence & coverage

Milan: all nine Municipi.

This release maps documented ERP addresses across the whole Comune di Milano, including areas outside the boulevard ring. It is based on the Comune di Milano’s Elenco stabili ERP register, which records the managing body for each listed address.

Sources and method

Each marker comes from the 2022 ERP register and links back to its source page. The address is retained as written in that register. Coordinates, Municipio and NIL are matched to the Comune di Milano’s civic-coordinate open dataset, updated in October 2026 and released under CC BY 4.0. Entries without an unambiguous active civic match are not plotted.

What a marker means

A marker represents a documented building entrance or civic address, managed by MM or ALER in the source register. It does not show flats, households, availability, current tenancy or a dwelling count. Multiple records at one entrance can be separate source entries.

Coverage and limits

The register is citywide but dated. It may not reflect later sales, redevelopment, demolitions or management changes, and it does not systematically cover private subsidised housing, cooperatives or former public stock. Absence from this map does not establish that social housing is absent.

Area layers

At a wider zoom, the map shows the nine Municipi. Zoom in to see the 88 Nuclei di Identità Locale (NIL), from Comune di Milano open data.
