Evidence & coverage

How to read this atlas.

Social Housing in Europe is a city-by-city atlas of documented social-housing addresses. Each city is published from its own public records and has its own sources, dates and limits. This page currently presents the first city dataset; it does not represent a Europe-wide inventory.

What a marker means

A marker represents a documented building entrance or address from a public housing register. It does not show apartments, households, availability, current tenancy or ownership. Absence from this map does not establish that social housing is absent.

Coverage and method

Records are transcribed from the city-specific registers listed below and matched to public civic coordinates where available. Addresses are normalised for mapping, but source wording is retained in each record where supplied. The dashed boundary is a study-area aid, not an administrative or legal boundary.

Limits

Registers can be incomplete, revised or dated. Changes in management, sales, redevelopment and demolition may not yet be reflected. The atlas does not infer dwelling totals and does not systematically cover private subsidised housing, cooperatives or former public stock. New city datasets will have different coverage and must be assessed through their own source notes.

City dataset sources

The source links attached to individual markers identify the register and, where known, the source page for that address. Consult them before treating a point as current or comprehensive.
