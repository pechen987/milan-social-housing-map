Evidence & method · October 2026

Published public-housing properties in Rome.

This release maps 782 distinct point locations from Roma Capitale’s public Atlante del Patrimonio, filtered to Edilizia Residenziale Pubblica (ERP). The atlas supplies the property classification, address, managing body and coordinates.

Coverage limits

The atlas currently returns 790 ERP records. Two have no published geometry, and six repeat an identical point and address, so 782 distinct locations are mapped. A marker identifies a public-property record and may represent a building or complex; it does not identify individual dwellings, tenants, availability or every form of affordable housing in the city.
