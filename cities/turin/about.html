Turin: building inventory in progress

One building is documented so far: Viale dei Mughetti 20. ATC’s 2021–2024 report identifies one building with three staircases and 61 social homes, owned by the city and managed by ATC. This is a historical source observation, not a current occupancy check.

The coordinate is OpenStreetMap’s scala A civic entrance, not a rooftop position. Other entrances are not separate buildings. Coordinate data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Housing facts are paraphrased from the linked ATC report; the report itself is not redistributed.

Piazza Montale 8 is excluded because the report describes a community hub and post office, not social housing. Further municipal and ATC records and all districts still require research. Absence of markers does not establish absence of social housing.
