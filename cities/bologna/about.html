Evidence & method · October 2026

ACER public-housing buildings in Bologna.

This release maps 630 building footprints from the Comune di Bologna’s public ACER map service. Markers use the centroid of each official building polygon.

Coverage limits

The layer identifies ACER public-housing buildings, not individual apartments, tenancies or vacancies. It may not cover every municipal, co-operative or private subsidised home in Bologna.
