ACER public-housing buildings in Bologna.

The source contains 630 features with 579 distinct building codes. Eight exact duplicate features were consolidated using both geometry and building code, leaving 622 mapped records. Of these, 536 have unambiguous building identities; 86 remain under review because multiple geometries share a building code. Markers retain source footprint centroids. Old links to merged records still resolve.

Coverage limits

The layer documents buildings containing ACER housing. Condominium and leased-property categories do not establish that every dwelling has social tenure. Source matching was checked on 15 October 2026; the source stock date is unknown. It may not cover every municipal, co-operative or private subsidised home in Bologna.
