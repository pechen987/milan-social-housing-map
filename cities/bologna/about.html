Evidence & method · October 2026

ACER public-housing buildings in Bologna.

The official layer contains 630 source features with 579 distinct building codes. Identity and footprint reconciliation leaves 576 source-verified buildings and six unresolved source features (582 mapped records). Repeated features were merged only when they share an official building code and every pair overlaps at least 75% of both footprints. All original share links are preserved.

Every marker is checked against its source polygon. Points that fell outside the polygon were moved inside it; these are building-location points, not entrances. Six features with codes 318, 320 and 1282 have substantially different geometries and remain under review. They must not be counted as six verified buildings.

Coverage limits

The layer documents buildings containing ACER housing. Condominium and leased-property categories do not establish that every dwelling has social tenure. Source matching was checked on 16 October 2026; the source stock date is unknown. It may not cover every municipal, co-operative or private subsidised home in Bologna.
