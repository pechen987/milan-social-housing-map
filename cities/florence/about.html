Evidence & method · October 2026

Published Casa S.p.A. housing addresses in Florence.

This release maps eight distinct Florence addresses listed as ALLOGGIO in one published Casa S.p.A. property register. It does not represent the full Florence ERP stock. Their positions are joined to the Comune di Firenze civic-address dataset.

Coverage limits

The source lists individual property units and this map collapses them to their distinct addresses. It is only the address-level subset contained in that register, not a census of all ERP homes, vacancies or all housing managed by Casa S.p.A.
