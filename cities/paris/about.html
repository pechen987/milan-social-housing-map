Sources & coverage

Paris social-housing programmes.

This map maps 3,876 programme locations compiled from 4,293 City of Paris social-housing programme records financed since 2001. It is a public programme register, not a complete or live inventory of every social-housing home in Paris.

Programme locations

City of Paris — Programmes de logements sociaux financés à Paris ↗

Each point is a programme location. A programme can contain multiple homes, and its finance year, provider, programme type and home count are retained in the downloadable data.

Areas

Arrondissements ↗ · Administrative quarters ↗

The map displays all 20 arrondissement borders at city scale and all 80 administrative-quarter borders when zoomed in.

Important limits

  • A mapped point means the programme appears in this source; it does not prove that a specific building is currently available, occupied, or the only social housing nearby.
  • Counts are programme locations, not homes, apartments or buildings.
  • Provider names are shown in individual cards and exports only. They are not used to colour or filter the main map, so cities remain comparable.
