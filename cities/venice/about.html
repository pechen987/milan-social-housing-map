Sources & coverage

Venice public-housing locations.

This first release maps 123 civic locations from 568 ATER Venezia dwelling entries in the Veneto Region’s 2013 register of dwellings excluded from sale. It is not a current or complete inventory of public or social housing in Venice.

ATER Veneto register

Regione del Veneto — DGR 1974/2013, Annex C1 ↗

The register lists 2,035 ATER Venezia dwellings across the province. The map includes the 568 entries whose locality is within the Comune di Venezia, then consolidates repeated dwelling entries at the same civic location.

Coordinates

Comune di Venezia civic coordinates ↗

Each plotted point was matched to the municipal civic-coordinate layer. Three source civics without an exact match are deliberately excluded rather than guessed.

Known limits

  • The source is from 2013; ownership, management, sale status and occupancy may have changed.
  • The register’s Venice entries are in Chirignago, Favaro Veneto, Marghera and Mestre. It should not be read as coverage of every public-housing building in the historic centre, islands or wider municipality.
  • Counts are dwellings in the source register and civic locations on the map, not a live count of apartments or availability.
